How it works

Four steps. About an hour of your time before the plan exists, and a link you check whenever you want after that.

  1. A free 30-minute call

    You talk, we listen and ask the awkward questions. At the end we tell you the two or three things that matter most in your situation, and whether a full plan is worth paying for. Roughly one in five people who call us are told they do not need us yet. That call costs you nothing either way.

  2. The data session

    If you go ahead, you get one structured form — income, spending, assets, loans, policies, goals. Best estimates are fine; we chase the gaps. This is the only part that takes effort, and it takes about an hour.

  3. Your written plan, within 7 days

    We build it, then walk you through it live, section by section, until you can explain it back to us. You get the document, the numbers behind it, and a prioritised action list — what to do this month, this quarter, this year.

  4. Reviews, and a dashboard that stays current

    Your plan goes onto a private dashboard that we update every month. We review formally every quarter and whenever something big happens — a job change, a baby, a house, a market fall.

The dashboard

This is the part most people have not seen before, so it is worth explaining.

Every client gets a private web link. No app, no password to forget, no spreadsheet. Open it on your phone and you see this month’s position:

  • Net worth — everything you own against everything you owe
  • Monthly surplus — what is genuinely left after real spending
  • Six health ratios — savings, liquidity, leverage, solvency, debt-to-income and financial-asset ratio, each marked on track or needs attention
  • Asset allocation — what you hold today against your target mix, with the rupee amount to add or trim per asset class
  • Retirement projection — where your current pace lands against the corpus you need
  • Your action plan — what is still open, in priority order

It carries a client code, never your name, and the link is unguessable. Nobody can browse to another client’s page, and we do not publish the list.

What we never ask for

  • Your bank or broker passwords
  • Transaction or trading rights on your accounts
  • Custody of a single rupee

You place every transaction yourself. We are the people who tell you what to place and why — which is a very different job from the one most of the industry does.

What we need from you

Gather what you can. Missing documents are normal and not a reason to delay.

Income

Salary slip or latest ITR, plus any rent, dividend or business income. For RSUs or ESOPs, the grant and vesting schedule.

What you own

A consolidated mutual fund statement (CAS), demat holdings, EPF and PPF balances, FDs, property, gold.

What you owe

Outstanding amount, interest rate and EMI for each loan, including credit card balances you carry over.

Policies

Every life and health policy, including the ones a relative sold you and the ones you have forgotten the name of.

Spending

A realistic monthly figure by category. If you do not track it, we have a one-page method that takes twenty minutes.

Goals

Roughly what you want, roughly when. Refining these is part of the work, so vague answers are acceptable to start.
