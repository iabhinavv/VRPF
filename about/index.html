About VRPF

VR Personal Finance is a financial planning practice based in New Chandigarh, working with individuals and families across India and with NRIs planning around India. We have planned for more than 100 households, and we have never earned a rupee of commission.

Why we work this way

Most people in India get their financial advice from someone who is paid when they buy something. The bank relationship manager, the insurance agent, the family friend with an agency code. Not all of them give bad advice. But every one of them is paid more for some recommendations than for others, and that shapes what gets recommended, often without anyone intending harm.

The result is familiar: endowment policies sold as investments, five funds that hold the same stocks, and nobody at all responsible for the question of whether you will have enough at sixty.

We built VRPF to do the job the other way round. You pay us a flat fee for the work. We are paid by nobody else, which means we have no reason to prefer one product over another, and no reason to care whether you buy anything at all. Sometimes the right advice is to cancel something, or to pay off a loan, or to do nothing. Those answers are free for us to give.

What we are, precisely

We think being exact about this is part of the service, so here it is without the marketing gloss.

We are a financial planning and money-coaching practice. We do goal planning, cash-flow and budgeting work, savings-rate design, asset-allocation guidance at the asset-class level, insurance-needs analysis, debt strategy, tax-aware structuring and retirement projections. Our team holds NISM certifications, which are the securities-market examinations administered by the National Institute of Securities Markets.

We are not registered with SEBI as an Investment Adviser, and we are not a mutual fund distributor. That means we do not recommend specific securities or schemes, we do not manage or hold your money, and we do not execute transactions on your behalf. If your situation needs security-specific advice, we will tell you plainly and help you find a SEBI-registered adviser.

A NISM certification is an examination, not a registration, and we will never present it as more than that. Read the full disclaimer →

How we measure ourselves

Not by assets under management, because we do not manage any. We look at whether clients can explain their own plan back to us, whether the action list actually gets done, and whether the savings rate holds through a year with a holiday and a wedding in it.

The dashboard exists for the same reason. A plan nobody looks at is a document, not a plan.
