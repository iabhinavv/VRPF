Financial planner in Chandigarh, Mohali and Panchkula

We are based in New Chandigarh and work with families across the Tricity — Chandigarh, Mohali, Panchkula and Zirakpur — in person or over video, whichever you prefer.

Most financial advice available locally is attached to a product. Somebody is paid when you buy a policy or a fund, and the conversation shapes itself around that. We are paid a flat fee by you and nothing by anybody else, which is a different conversation entirely.

Who we work with locally

  • IT and services professionals in the Mohali and Chandigarh IT parks, often with RSUs or ESOPs from a parent company abroad
  • Doctors and professionals in private practice across the Tricity, with income that does not arrive like a salary
  • Business owners in and around the industrial areas, whose personal finances are tangled with the firm’s
  • Government and PSU employees planning around pension rules, NPS and a fixed retirement date
  • Families with a member abroad — a common Punjab pattern, and one that needs the NRI side handled properly

Why local still matters

The whole engagement works remotely, and most of our clients are elsewhere. But some people want to sit across a table for the first conversation, particularly when parents are involved in the decision or when the family’s finances span two generations. You are welcome to do that.

Questions from local clients

Do I have to come to the office?

No. Everything can be done over video, and most clients never visit. The office is there if you would rather meet in person.

Do you work in Hindi or Punjabi?

Yes. Conversations happen in English, Hindi or Punjabi, whichever is more comfortable — and that matters when parents are part of the discussion.

Can you help my parents with their retirement money?

Yes, and it is common. Retired-parent planning is a different job from yours — income from a fixed corpus, health cover that is getting expensive, nominations and a will. We can plan for both generations together.

Are you a SEBI-registered investment adviser?

No. We are a financial planning and coaching practice, and we do not recommend specific securities or manage money. Read exactly what we do and do not do →
