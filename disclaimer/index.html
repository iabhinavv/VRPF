Plain language, because this is the page where plain language matters most.

What VRPF provides

VR Personal Finance (“VRPF”) provides financial planning and money-coaching services to individuals and families. Our work covers goal setting and costing, cash-flow and budgeting analysis, savings-rate design, asset-allocation guidance at the asset-class level, insurance-needs analysis, debt strategy, tax-aware structuring and retirement projections, delivered as a written plan with ongoing reviews.

What VRPF does not provide

  • We are not registered with SEBI as an Investment Adviser under the SEBI (Investment Advisers) Regulations, 2013, and we do not hold ourselves out as investment advisers.
  • We are not a mutual fund distributor and hold no AMFI registration number. We do not distribute or sell any financial product.
  • We do not recommend specific securities. We do not advise on which particular share, mutual fund scheme, bond or insurance policy to buy or sell.
  • We do not manage money or hold custody. We do not operate your accounts, hold power of attorney, request your passwords, or execute transactions on your behalf.
  • We do not provide legal, accounting or statutory tax services. We do not file returns or issue tax opinions. Work with your chartered accountant or lawyer for those.
  • We do not advise on the tax or regulatory position of any country other than India.

Members of our team hold certifications from the National Institute of Securities Markets (NISM). A NISM certification is an examination qualification. It is not a registration with SEBI and confers no authority to provide investment advice as defined under the Regulations.
