Talk to us

The first call is free, lasts about thirty minutes, and carries no obligation of any kind. Booking it is the fastest way to get a straight answer about your situation.

What happens on the call

  1. You describe where you are — income, what you own, what worries you
  2. We ask the questions that usually get skipped
  3. We tell you the two or three things that matter most in your case
  4. If a full plan would help, we explain the scope and quote a flat fee. If it would not, we say so

You keep whatever you learn, whether or not you become a client.

Before you book

A few things worth knowing, so the call is useful.

Bring rough numbers

Approximate income, approximate monthly spending, roughly what you have invested. Precision is not needed at this stage.

Both partners, ideally

If finances are shared, the call works far better with both people on it. Plans made by one person rarely survive contact with the other.

Nothing to prepare

No documents, no forms, no spreadsheet. Those come later, and only if you decide to go ahead.
