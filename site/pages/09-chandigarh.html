Questions from local clients

Do I have to come to the office?

No. Everything can be done over video, and most clients never visit. The office is there if you would rather meet in person.

Do you work in Hindi or Punjabi?

Yes. Conversations happen in English, Hindi or Punjabi, whichever is more comfortable — and that matters when parents are part of the discussion.

Can you help my parents with their retirement money?

Yes, and it is common. Retired-parent planning is a different job from yours — income from a fixed corpus, health cover that is getting expensive, nominations and a will. We can plan for both generations together.

Are you a SEBI-registered investment adviser?

No. We are a financial planning and coaching practice, and we do not recommend specific securities or manage money. Read exactly what we do and do not do →
