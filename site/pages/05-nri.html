What NRIs come to us with

Accounts that were never converted

A resident savings account left running after you moved is not permitted once your status changes. Most people discover this years late. We go through what should be NRE, what should be NRO, and what should be closed.

Money sitting idle in India

Large NRE fixed deposits earning less than inflation, because investing from abroad felt complicated. It is more paperwork, not more difficulty.

A flat bought for the wrong reason

Indian property bought as an emotional anchor often returns less than it costs, once you count maintenance, vacancy and the tax on repatriating a sale. We price it honestly.

Supporting parents without structure

Money goes home every month with no plan behind it. We turn it into a costed goal, including their medical cover and what happens if a crisis arrives while you are abroad.

Two retirement systems, no single view

A 401(k) or pension there, EPF and property here. Neither picture is complete alone, and the retirement decision depends on both.

What we cover, and what we do not

We cover the structure of your India-side finances: which accounts you should hold, how your India investments should be allocated across asset classes, how much of your family obligation to fund and from where, what your India property is really doing for you, what cover your dependants need, and how the India side feeds the retirement number in whichever country you choose.

We do not give tax opinions for your country of residence, file returns, or advise on specific securities in any market. For a US or UK tax position you need a qualified adviser there, and we will coordinate with them rather than guess.

Worth knowing: some Indian mutual funds restrict investment from US and Canada-based NRIs because of FATCA reporting. This changes what is practical for you, and we work it out before we build the plan, not after.

Common questions

Can you manage my investments from India while I am abroad?

No, and that is deliberate. We never take custody or transaction rights on anyone’s accounts. We tell you what to do and you execute it — which also keeps your money entirely under your own control and visible to your own bank.

I am moving back to India next year. When should we start?

Ideally six to twelve months before you move. Several of the useful decisions — selling foreign holdings while still non-resident, timing large transfers across financial years, understanding your RNOR window — stop being available once you land.

Do you handle FEMA and repatriation paperwork?

We explain the rules and the sequence, and tell you what your bank or chartered accountant will need. We do not file documents on your behalf.

Is the fee payable from abroad?

Yes. We will confirm the exact method on the call.
