What usually turns out to be true

Five patterns we see in almost every high-salary plan we open.

The raise was absorbed, not saved

A promotion lifts spending within about four months unless the increment is automated into investing first. We find the number that should have gone up and make it go up.

RSUs are treated as a lottery ticket

They are salary paid in your employer’s stock, already taxed as a perquisite, and they quietly make your portfolio a bet on one company you also depend on for income. How ESOPs and RSUs are taxed →

Six funds, one portfolio

Most multi-fund portfolios we review hold largely the same stocks. More funds is not more diversification — it is more statements.

Insurance is doing the wrong job

A ULIP or endowment bought for tax saving is usually a poor investment and thin cover. We calculate the term cover your family actually needs and separate the two jobs.

Tax is handled every March

Section 80C in a panic is not tax planning. Regime choice, NPS, capital-gains timing and RSU sale planning are decisions made across the year, not in the last week of it.

No one has run the retirement number

Most people earning well have never calculated the corpus their lifestyle needs. It is usually larger than expected, and the gap is usually still closeable. Work out your number →

What the engagement covers for a salaried earner

  • Take-home mapping — your CTC broken into what actually lands, including the EPF, gratuity and employer NPS most people forget they own
  • A savings rate you will keep — automated on salary day, not left to whatever survives the month
  • Equity compensation — vesting schedule mapped, concentration risk measured, and a written rule for when to sell so the decision is not emotional
  • Old regime against new — computed on your numbers, not on a generic calculator
  • Term and health cover — the amounts, and whether your employer cover is enough on the day you leave the employer
  • Goal mapping — house, children, parents, sabbatical, retirement, each costed and dated
  • A retirement projection — and an honest answer on whether an early one is realistic for you

If you are thinking about FIRE

We will run the number properly: your real expenses inflated forward, a withdrawal rate that survives a bad first decade, and health cover for the forty years after your employer stops providing it.

Sometimes the answer is yes, later than you hoped. Sometimes the answer is that you do not want to retire, you want to change jobs — which costs far less and we will say so.

Common questions

I am already investing through SIPs. What would you add?

Usually subtraction before addition. The first deliverable is normally fewer funds, a correct emergency fund, the right term cover, and a savings rate tied to your goals rather than to a number you picked in 2019.

Can you help with my RSUs and ESOPs?

We help you understand how they are taxed, how much of your net worth is sitting in one employer, and what a sensible sell-down rule looks like. We do not tell you to buy or sell a specific share — that is security-specific advice, which needs SEBI registration we do not hold. We work at the level of concentration, tax and cash flow.

Is this worth it on a ₹20 lakh salary?

Often yes, because the decisions you make in your early thirties compound for thirty years. But we will tell you on the free call if your situation is simple enough to handle yourself — and if it is, we will tell you what to do and send you on your way.

Do you work with people outside Chandigarh?

Yes. Most of our salaried clients are in Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Pune, Gurugram and Mumbai, and the entire process runs over video.
