The problem is rarely income. It is that nobody owns the whole picture.

If any of these sound like your last twelve months, you are the person we built this for.

“My salary doubled and I have nothing to show for it”

Lifestyle rises to meet income unless something stops it. Without a written savings rate, a raise disappears in about four months.

“I have six SIPs and I don’t know why”

Funds get added, never removed. Most portfolios we open are four funds holding the same thirty stocks, and one that should have been sold years ago.

“Someone sold me a policy I don’t understand”

Insurance bought as an investment is usually both — badly. We tell you what it costs you to keep it, and what it costs you to leave.

“I keep meaning to sit down and work it out”

You will not. Not because you are lazy, but because it takes a weekend you do not have and a spreadsheet you do not enjoy. That is the job you are hiring us for.

What you actually get

One engagement. Everything below is included — there is no upsell at the end.

1. A full financial health check

Income, spending, every asset, every loan, every policy, in one place. Six standard health ratios — savings, liquidity, leverage, solvency, debt-to-income and financial-asset ratio — scored against the ranges a planner actually uses.

2. A written plan, in 7 days

Not a sales deck. A document that names your goals, the amount each one needs, the monthly figure that gets you there, and the order to do things in. Written in English, not jargon.

3. Your own live dashboard

A private link, updated every month: net worth, surplus, asset allocation against target, retirement projection, and the actions still open. No app to download, no login to forget. See what it looks like.

4. Reviews as life changes

A job change, a baby, a house, a market drop. Plans fail when nobody revisits them. We revisit them with you — quarterly, and whenever something big happens.

How we are paid, in one paragraph

You pay us a flat planning fee. That is the only money we make.

We do not earn commission from any mutual fund, insurer, bank or broker. We do not sell products. We do not hold your money, and we never ask for your passwords or transaction rights — you keep control of every rupee, and you place every transaction yourself.

This matters more than it sounds. A large part of the Indian industry is paid by the people whose products get recommended. When the person advising you is paid by the product, you are not the customer — you are the distribution.

Questions people ask before booking

Are you SEBI-registered investment advisers?

No, and we will not pretend otherwise. We are a financial planning and coaching practice. We help you with goals, cash flow, savings rate, asset-allocation ranges, insurance cover and retirement projections. We do not recommend specific stocks or schemes, we do not manage your money, and we do not execute transactions for you. If your situation needs security-specific advice, we will tell you to go to a SEBI-registered investment adviser — and we will help you pick a good one.

How much does it cost?

A flat fee, quoted on the first call once we know what your situation needs. It does not change with how much money you have, because our work does not. We explain the number and the scope before you commit to anything. More on fees →

How long does it take?

A 30-minute first call. If we go ahead, a detailed data-gathering session, then your written plan within 7 days. Your dashboard goes live with the plan and is updated monthly.

Do I have to be in Chandigarh?

No. Most of our clients are elsewhere in India or abroad, and the whole process works over video. If you are in Chandigarh, Mohali or Panchkula, you are welcome to come to the office.

I already invest. Is this still useful?

Usually more useful, not less. People who already invest tend to have more moving parts and more duplication. The first thing we often do is reduce the number of things you own, not add to it.

What do you need from me?

One structured form and about an hour of your time. Statements and policy documents where you have them, best estimates where you do not. We do the assembling.
